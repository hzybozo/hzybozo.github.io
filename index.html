COMMISSION INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before starting a Roblox design commission.

What do you design? +

I primarily design Roblox interfaces, menus, HUDs, shops, inventories, buttons, popups and other visual UI elements. Custom requirements can be discussed before the commission begins.

Do Advanced and Expert include tweens? +

Yes. Advanced and Expert include Roblox tweens and interactive animation. Basic does not include Roblox tweens.

Are GBP and Robux charged together? +

No. You pay using either the GBP price or the Robux price. They are alternative payment methods and are never combined.

Are the Robux prices after tax? +

Yes. The Robux figures shown on this website represent the amount required after Roblox tax.

How does payment work? +

Basic requires 100% upfront payment. Advanced requires 70% upfront payment. Expert requires 50% upfront payment.

Can I provide references? +

Yes. References, existing branding, colour palettes and examples can be provided so the design can be tailored to your project.

Can pricing be negotiated? +

Yes. Pricing can be discussed through DMs. Larger commissions, multiple frames and unusual requirements can receive a custom quote.

How do I start? +

Contact me through Discord at hzybozo and include what you need, how many frames are required and any references you have.
