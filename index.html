COMMISSION INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you should know before starting a Roblox design commission.

What do you design? +

I primarily design Roblox interfaces, menus, HUDs, shops, inventories, buttons, popups and other visual UI systems. The exact scope can be discussed before a commission begins.

Do you also animate the UI? +

Roblox tweens are included with the Advanced and Expert tiers. Basic focuses on the visual design and does not include Roblox tweens. More complex scripting can be discussed separately.

Are GBP and Robux charged together? +

No. You pay using either the GBP price or the Robux price. They are alternative payment methods and are never combined.

Are the Robux prices after tax? +

Yes. The Robux prices shown on this website represent the amount required after Roblox marketplace tax.

How does payment work? +

Basic requires 100% upfront payment. Advanced requires 70% upfront payment. Expert requires 50% upfront payment. The remaining balance is handled according to the agreed commission terms.

Can I request a custom design? +

Yes. References, existing branding, colour palettes and specific requirements can be provided before work begins.

Can prices be negotiated? +

Yes. Pricing can be discussed in DMs. Larger commissions, multiple frames and projects with unusual requirements may receive a custom quote.

How do I start a commission? +

Contact me through Discord at hzybozo and include what you need designed, the number of frames, references and your preferred tier.
