COMMISSION INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you should know before starting a Roblox design commission.

What do you design? +

I primarily design Roblox interfaces, menus, HUDs, shop interfaces, inventory systems, buttons and other visual UI elements.

Do you also animate the UI? +

Roblox tweens are included with the Advanced and Expert tiers. Basic focuses on the visual design and does not include Roblox tweens.

What is included in Basic? +

Basic focuses on the visual design itself. Roblox tweens are not included in this tier.

What is included in Advanced? +

Advanced includes a more detailed visual treatment and Roblox tweens designed to make the interface feel responsive and interactive.

What makes Expert different? +

Expert provides the highest level of detail and production quality, includes Roblox tweens and has a guaranteed one-day timeframe per frame.

Are GBP and Robux charged together? +

No. You pay using either the GBP price or the Robux price. They are alternative payment methods and are never combined.

Are the Robux prices after tax? +

Yes. The Robux prices displayed on this website are the amounts required after Roblox marketplace tax.

How does payment work? +

Basic requires 100% upfront payment. Advanced requires 70% upfront payment. Expert requires 50% upfront payment.

Can I request a custom design? +

Yes. References, existing branding, colour palettes and specific requirements can be provided before work begins.

Can prices be negotiated? +

Yes. Pricing can be discussed in DMs. Larger commissions, multiple frames and unusual requirements may receive a custom quote.

How do I start a commission? +

Contact me through Discord at hzybozo. Include what you need designed, the number of frames, any references and your preferred pricing tier.
